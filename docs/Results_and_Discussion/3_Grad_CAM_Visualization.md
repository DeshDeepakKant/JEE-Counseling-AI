# Results and Discussion: Grad-CAM Visualization

## Exploring Model Interpretability with Grad-CAM

Deep Learning models are frequently treated as "black boxes" in medical diagnosis. Medical experts and clinicians strictly require justification corresponding to diagnostic predictions (why did the model classify this image as a tumor?).

The authors utilized **Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping)** to validate that the neural network authentically recognized the shapes and edges of cancer formations, rather than just randomly mapping correlations.

**Grad-CAM Benefits:**
1. Allows radiologists to spot patterns and cross-verify SwarmFusionDNN predictions.
2. Increases the overall trustworthiness of automated diagnosis systems.
3. Provides targeted tumor region visualizations, crucial for clinical planning and pre-surgical tasks.

### Evaluation Takeaways:
Across the experiments conducted on the Br35H and Figshare test splits:
- High-intensity zones generated by Grad-CAM precisely aligned with the visually noticeable brain mass/pathology. 
- No spurious artifacts (skull edges, imaging watermarks, natural sulci shapes) incorrectly shifted the activation focus. 
- The dense overlapping heatmap accurately covered different variations of Meningiomas, Gliomas, and Pituitary tumors irrespective of size positioning or complexity.
